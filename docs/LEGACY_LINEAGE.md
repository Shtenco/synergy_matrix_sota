# Legacy lineage

Historical cyclic and matrix-style models are preserved as research provenance. They must not be deleted or silently rewritten.

Examples of historical lines to retain when imported or referenced:

- 10-participant / 100-unit queue experiments;
- cyclic order `0 -> 1 -> ... -> 9 -> 0`;
- repeated-pass and independent-tranche experiments;
- staggered maturity examples such as `100/88/76/64/52/44/36/28/20/12` weeks;
- 120-week participation simulations;
- 200-participant scale scenarios;
- token-price, vesting and liquidity experiments connected to the older matrix idea.

## Canonical reinterpretation

The useful surviving mechanisms are:

- recurring savings discipline;
- independent tranches;
- explicit duration;
- revolving sequential credit;
- liquidity and maturity matching;
- separation of principal from realized revenue.

Historical payout or token-price mechanics remain `LEGACY` or `EXPERIMENTAL` unless independently revalidated under the current Financial OS accounting rules.

## Non-circularity rule

A participant's current contribution must not be presented as independent external profit or as a self-funding source for an economically simultaneous obligation to that same participant. Funding eligibility must be computed from the actual pool state and contractually available capital.

## Evidence status

Every migrated historical mechanism should record:

- source repository/file/chat artifact;
- original assumptions;
- reproduction status;
- current evidence status;
- current accounting interpretation;
- known negative controls.
